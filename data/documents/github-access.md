# GitHub Access Policy

## Who gets access
Engineering employees are added to the northstar-eng GitHub organisation automatically on their first day. Other employees may request read access to specific repositories through AegisDesk with their manager's approval.

## Contractors
Contractors need a Northstar sponsor (an engineering manager) who approves the request. Contractor access expires after 90 days and must be renewed by the sponsor.

## Security requirements
Sign-in uses Northstar SSO, and a hardware security key is required for all members. Personal access tokens expire after 30 days. Forking private repositories to personal accounts is not allowed.
