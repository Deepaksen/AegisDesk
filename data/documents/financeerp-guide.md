# FinanceERP User Guide

## Signing in
FinanceERP is at erp.northstar.example and uses Northstar SSO. Access is limited to the finance department and must be approved by your manager (see the Application Access Policy).

## Roles
FinanceERP has three roles: AP Clerk (enters supplier invoices), Analyst (read-only reporting) and Controller (approves invoices and closes periods). New finance employees receive the Analyst role unless their manager requests another role.

## Invoice approval
Supplier invoices above EUR 10,000 must be approved by a Controller before payment. Invoices below that amount are approved by the AP Clerk's team lead.

## Month-end close
Postings to the previous month are locked at 18:00 on the 3rd business day of each month. Corrections after that require a Controller journal entry.
