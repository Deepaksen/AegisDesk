# Information Security Policy

## Data classification
Northstar information is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Confidential information (for example financial results before publication) is shared only with named teams. Restricted information (for example production customer data and credentials) is available only to named individuals with a business need.

## Reporting security incidents
Report suspected security incidents to security@northstar.example or through AegisDesk within 1 hour of noticing them. Report phishing emails with the "Report Phishing" button in Outlook; do not forward them.

## Devices and removable media
USB mass-storage devices are blocked on all laptops. Screens must lock after 5 minutes of inactivity. Full-disk encryption is mandatory and enforced by IT.

## Acceptable use
Never share your password or multi-factor codes with anyone, including IT staff. Company data must not be copied to personal devices or personal cloud storage.
