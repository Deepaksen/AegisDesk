# Employee IT Handbook

## Contacting the IT service desk
The IT service desk is open Monday to Friday, 07:00 to 19:00 CET. Employees can reach it through AegisDesk, by phone on extension 4357 (HELP), or by email at itsupport@northstar.example. Outside these hours, only P1 incidents (a whole team unable to work, or a security incident) are handled by the on-call engineer.

## Your equipment
Every employee receives a standard laptop, one external monitor and a docking station. Engineering staff may request a second monitor. Laptops are refreshed every 4 years; you will be contacted by IT when your device is due. Do not install hardware yourself: request peripherals through AegisDesk.

A loaner laptop is available within one business day if your laptop is in repair. Loaners must be returned within 5 business days of your own laptop being repaired.

## Lost or stolen devices
Report a lost or stolen laptop or phone within 24 hours through AegisDesk or by calling extension 4357. IT will remotely lock and wipe the device. Delayed reporting is a breach of the Information Security Policy.

## Leaving Northstar
All equipment must be returned on or before your last working day. Accounts are disabled at 18:00 on the last working day. Devices that are not returned are marked as retrieval pending and followed up by HR.
