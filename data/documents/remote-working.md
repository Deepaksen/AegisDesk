# Remote Working Policy

## Eligibility
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week, agreed with their manager. Contractors follow the terms of their contract.

## Secure remote work
The VPN is required to access internal systems from outside Northstar offices. Public Wi-Fi may be used only with the VPN connected. Do not work on Confidential or Restricted information in public places where screens can be seen.

## Working from abroad
Working from another country for more than 5 consecutive days needs approval from your manager and HR. Tell IT at least 10 days before travelling so that sign-ins from abroad are not blocked.

## Home office equipment
Employees receive a one-time home office allowance of EUR 300 for a chair or desk. IT provides the laptop, monitor and docking station; other peripherals are requested through AegisDesk.
