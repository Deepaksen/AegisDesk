# Password Reset Guide

## Password rules
Northstar passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Passphrases of four or more unrelated words are recommended. Passwords do not expire on a schedule; they must be changed immediately if you suspect they have been exposed. Reusing any of your last 10 passwords is not allowed.

## Resetting your password yourself
Go to password.northstar.example, enter your employee email address, and approve the Okta Verify push on your phone. Choose a new password that follows the rules above. The new password works everywhere within 5 minutes.

## Account lockout
After 5 failed sign-in attempts your account is locked for 15 minutes. Wait 15 minutes and try again, or reset your password using self-service.

## If self-service does not work
If you no longer have access to Okta Verify (for example a new phone), call the service desk on extension 4357. The service desk will verify your identity with your manager before resetting multi-factor authentication. Service desk staff will never ask you for your password.
